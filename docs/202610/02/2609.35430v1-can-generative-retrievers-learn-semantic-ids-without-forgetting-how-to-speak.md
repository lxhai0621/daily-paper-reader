## 摘要
生成式检索（GR）通过生成文档语义标识符（SID）实现端到端检索。然而，仅针对检索进行微调可能使预训练语言模型过度专门化于SID预测，显著扭曲其自然语言分布，并限制其适用于既需检索文档又需生成自然语言响应的交互式系统。我们提出SpeakGR，一种双目标框架，可在学习SID的同时保留语言生成能力。它将监督式SID学习与保持说话能力的正则化相结合：一种同策略蒸馏目标，利用原始文本词表上的前向KL，在由学生模型生成的前缀上使当前模型与原始模型的冻结副本对齐。我们进一步提出Adaptive SpeakGR，它根据观测到的语言漂移动态调整保持强度。与仅使用SFT相比，SpeakGR在MS MARCO上将WikiText-2前向KL降低81.3-93.8%，在Natural Questions（NQ）上降低81.2-85.2%，同时在三种不同LLM上保持有效检索。Adaptive SpeakGR在大多数设置下进一步提升检索效果，同时保持比仅使用SFT低得多的语言漂移。

## Abstract
Generative retrieval (GR) enables end-to-end retrieval by generating document semantic identifiers (SIDs). However, retrieval-only fine-tuning can over-specialize pretrained language models to SID prediction, substantially distorting their natural-language distribution and limiting their suitability for interactive systems that must both retrieve documents and generate natural-language responses. We introduce SpeakGR, a dual-objective framework that learns SIDs while preserving language generation. It combines supervised SID learning with speak-preserving regularization: an on-policy distillation objective that aligns the current model with a frozen copy of the original model on student-generated prefixes using forward KL over the original text vocabulary. We further propose Adaptive SpeakGR, which dynamically adjusts the preservation strength based on observed language drift. Compared with SFT-only, SpeakGR reduces WikiText-2 forward KL by 81.3-93.8% on MS MARCO and 81.2-85.2% on Natural Questions (NQ) while retaining effective retrieval across three different LLMs. Adaptive SpeakGR further improves retrieval over SpeakGR in most settings while maintaining substantially lower language drift than SFT-only.
