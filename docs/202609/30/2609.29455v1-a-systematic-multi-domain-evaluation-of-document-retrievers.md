## 摘要
文档检索是许多现代 AI 系统的重要组成部分，直接影响其在下游任务中的有效性、鲁棒性和公平性。近年来，尽管出现了越来越多的检索器，但文献中的比较研究通常范围有限，或只聚焦于单一基准、领域或模型家族。这种碎片化使得人们难以就文档检索器的相对优势、劣势和权衡得出可靠结论。为填补这一空白，我们对文档检索器进行了大规模实证评估，涵盖三个家族（稀疏、稠密和基于扩展的方法），在七个 IR 数据集上评估了 33 个检索器，分析检索质量、运行时间和失败点。我们没有对每个模型单独调参，而是在可由其公开文档重建的配置和统一计算预算下，对每个检索器进行开箱即用式评估。结果表明，NV-Embed-v2 在七个数据集中的四个上取得了最强性能，但代价是显著的查询延迟。在稀疏检索器中，我们发现 SPLADE-v3 尽管延迟低得多，却能媲美表现最佳的方法，甚至在 MS MARCO 上取得最高分。在遵循指令的数据集上，GritLM 表现最佳。最后，对检索器失败点的分析揭示了模型和家族之间的差异，这表明检索性能仍有尚未实现的提升空间。

## Abstract
Document retrieval is a crucial component of many modern AI systems, directly influencing their effectiveness, robustness, and fairness in downstream tasks. While recent years have seen a growing number of retrievers, comparative studies in the literature are typically limited in scope or focused on singular benchmarks, domains, or model families. This fragmentation makes it difficult to draw reliable conclusions about the relative strengths, weaknesses, and trade-offs of document retrievers. To address this gap, we conduct a large-scale empirical evaluation of document retrievers, covering three families (sparse, dense, and expansion-based) and evaluating 33 retrievers across seven IR datasets, analyzing retrieval quality, runtime, and failure points. Rather than tuning each model individually, we evaluate every retriever off the shelf, under the configuration reconstructable from its public documentation and a uniform compute budget. Our results show that NV-Embed-v2 achieves the strongest performance on four of the seven datasets, albeit at the cost of substantial query latencies. Among sparse retrievers, we find that SPLADE-v3 rivals the top-performing approach despite much lower latency, and even achieves top scores on MS MARCO. On instruction-following datasets, GritLM delivers the best performance. Finally, an analysis of the retrievers' failure points reveals contrasts between models and families that indicate potential for unrealized gains in retrieval performance.
