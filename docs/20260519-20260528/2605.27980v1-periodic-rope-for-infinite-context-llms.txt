# Periodic RoPE for Infinite Context LLMs 

Simin Huo 

Shanghai Jiao Tong University 

Abstract 

The ability to process ultra-long contexts is crucial for large language models (LLMs) to perform long-horizon tasks. While recent ef-forts have extended context windows to 1M and beyond, model performance degrades when se-quence length exceeds the pre-trained range of positional encodings (e.g., RoPE), i.e., posi-tion exhaustion. This fundamental limitation must be overcome to achieve a truly infinite context. To address it, we propose Periodic RoPE (P-RoPE), a positional encoding mech-anism designed to circumvent this exhaustion. It operates in conjunction with sliding window attention (SWA) to capture local dependencies and relative positions within each window. This local layer is then complemented by a global attention layer with No Positional Encoding (NoPE), enabling unbounded interaction across the entire sequence without positional con-straints. By stacking these two types of lay-ers, the model avoids the need for positional extrapolation to generalize longer and theo-retically supports an infinite context window. Empirical results show that our model, Mini-Win, outperforms MiniMInd with standard GPT architectures in long-context efficiency and stability. Our work provides a possible pathway toward LLMs with genuine infinite-context understanding. The code is available at https://github.com/Cominder/miniwin.

To address it, we replace the causal convo-lution with SWA using ALiBi (Press et al., 2022), clamped to the window size W so that the linear distance penalty is non-zero only within [0 , W − 1] .This combination ((SWA + ALiBi) + (RTR-WA + NoPE)) eliminates the repetition degeneration and yields coherent text generation. Accordingly, we designed a periodic RoPE with a sliding window size W as its period to avoid exhaustion , which we refer to as Periodic RoPE (P-RopE). Finally, we get ((SWA + P-RoPE) + (GA + NoPE)) .

2 Related Work 

Long-context positional encoding. ROPE (Su et al., 2024) encodes relative offsets through rota-tion matrices; ALiBi (Press et al., 2022) adds a lin-ear penalty to attention logits. Both degrade beyond the training horizon. Interpolation methods such as Position Interpolation (Chen et al., 2023), YaRN (Peng et al., 2023a), and LongRoPE (Ding et al., 2024) extend the range but require fine-tuning and do not guarantee infinite context.N OPE (Kazemne-jad et al., 2023) drops positional encoding entirely and relies on the causal mask for order, showing competitive performance. 

Long-context LLMs. Several recent architec-tures aim for efficient long-context process-ing beyond standard Transformers. SWAN-GPT (Puvvada et al., 2025) interleaves NoPE global attention with RoPE-based sliding win-dow attention, achieving 128k extrapolation with-out long training. Samba(Ren et al., 2025) com-bines Mamba (SSM) with sliding window atten-tion, extrapolating from 4k to 1M tokens with sub-quadratic complexity. InfLLM(Xiao et al., 2024) and EM-LLM (Fountas et al., 2025) adopt training-free retrieval mechanisms—dynamic memory bank and event-cognition segmentation respectively, en-abling theoretically infinite context. 6 Limitations 

Scale. Due to limited computational resources, we trained only a mini model. Whether the P-ROPE will maintain the same advantage on models with hundreds of billions to trillions of parameters remains to be verified. 

Rigorous theoretical explanation. As shown in Case 2, P-R OPE produces the effective offset 

d−W rather than the true distance d, causing the key to appear closer than it actually is. Although we believe the model solved it in some way through learning, and experiments confirm that generation quality, formal proof is currently lacking to ex-plain why P-R OPE works and whether there are any risks. 

Window-size sensitivity. The periodic wrap-around rate is governed by W . Very small win-dows (e.g. W = 8 ) may limit the expressiveness of local attention, while very large windows increase KV-cache memory during decoding. We have not conducted a systematic ablation over W at scale. 

7 Conclusion 

We present MiniWin, a decoder-only Transformer that combines P-R OPE sliding window attention with NoPE global attention for infinite-context lan-guage modeling. Its core design is P-R OPE with a sliding window size W as its period, ensuring the positional encodings never exhaust. Experiments show competitive retrieval accuracy beyond the training length. This simple design may encourage further exploration of P-R OPE at scale. 4References
