## 摘要
大语言模型（LLMs）正在迅速改变材料科学和化学领域的研究人员发现、组织和利用科学知识的方式。本文分析了一系列由社区开发的LLM应用，旨在识别这些系统在整个科学研究生命周期中应用的新兴模式。我们将这些项目分为两个互补的类别：知识基础设施（Knowledge Infrastructure），即对科学信息进行结构化、检索、综合和验证的系统；以及行动系统（Action Systems），即在计算和实验环境中执行、协调或自动化科学工作的系统。提交的作品揭示了从单一用途的LLM工具向集成了检索、推理、工具使用和领域特定验证的多智能体工作流的转变。突出的主题包括作为基础架构的检索增强生成（RAG）、持久化的结构化知识表示、多模态和多语言科学输入，以及在实验室集成闭环系统方面取得的早期进展。总之，这些结果表明LLM正在从通用助手演变为用于科学推理和行动的可组合基础设施。这项工作提供了该转型的社区缩影，并为理解材料科学和化学中新兴的LLM赋能工作流提供了一个实用的分类法。

## Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) are rapidly changing how researchers in materials science and chemistry discover, organize, and act on scientific knowledge. This paper analyzes a broad set of community-developed LLM applications in an effort to identify emerging patterns in how these systems can be used across the scientific research lifecycle. We organize the projects into two complementary categories: Knowledge Infrastructure, systems that structure, retrieve, synthesize, and validate scientific information; and Action Systems, systems that execute, coordinate, or automate scientific work across computational and experimental environments. The submissions reveal a shift from single-purpose LLM tools toward integrated, multi-agent workflows that combine retrieval, reasoning, tool use, and domain-specific validation. Prominent themes include retrieval-augmented generation as grounding infrastructure, persistent structured knowledge representations, multimodal and multilingual scientific inputs, and early progress toward laboratory-integrated closed-loop systems. Together, these results suggest that LLMs are evolving from general-purpose assistants into composable infrastructure for scientific reasoning and action. This work provides a community snapshot of that transition and a practical taxonomy for understanding emerging LLM-enabled workflows in materials science and chemistry.

*   **应用限制**：许多项目高度依赖闭源模型，对于数据隐私敏感的工业应用可能存在限制。
*   **实验深度**：受限于黑客松的时间限制（2天），部分项目的消融实验和长程稳定性测试不足。
